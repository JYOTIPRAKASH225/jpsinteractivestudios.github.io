Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

In short: we don't collect any of your information. 🎉

City Voyager: World Run ("the game") is a mobile game made by JPS Interactive Studios ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it.

🔒 Information we collect

None. The game does not ask for, collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information. It has no accounts or logins, no advertising, no analytics or tracking, and no in-app purchases.

📵 Internet access

The game does not use the internet. It does not request Android's internet permission, so it cannot send anything from your device.

📱 Information stored on your device

Your game progress (completed goals, unlocked cities) and your settings (music, sounds, vibration, graphics) are saved only on your own device, so you can continue where you left off. This information never leaves your device. You can delete it at any time by clearing the game's storage in your phone's settings or by uninstalling the game.

📳 Permissions

The game asks for one permission: vibration, used for small vibrations when you bump into something or complete a goal. You can turn vibration off in the game's Settings.

🧒 Children

The game is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either.

🧩 Third parties

The game is made with the Unity game engine. The game's data-collection services (Unity Analytics, Crash Reporting and Performance Reporting) are turned off, and without internet access the game cannot send data anywhere. Google Play, which you use to download the game, has its own privacy policy.

📝 Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
